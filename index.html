Hypertrove

Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026

Hypertrove is a local-first strength-training app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It does not require an account, and it does not operate a server that collects or stores your workout, motion, or health data.

Data used by the app

Hypertrove may store workout plans, completed workout entries, exercise notes, set timing, Apple Watch motion samples, and exercise-specific calibration and review labels on your devices. With your permission, Hypertrove can access Apple Health workout and heart-rate information needed to link workouts and associate observations with a workout set. The Watch app can save a workout you start to Apple Health.

This data is used to provide the app's workout logging, analysis, and experimental motion-review features. Hypertrove does not sell this data and does not upload it to a developer-operated server.

Sharing and exports

You control exports from Hypertrove. Data leaves the app only when you choose an export or sharing action, such as sending a workout report or diagnostic motion file. The recipient and service you choose may process that export under their own privacy terms.

TestFlight and Apple services

Beta versions are distributed through Apple TestFlight. Apple may provide the developer with TestFlight crash reports, screenshots, written feedback, and basic testing metrics under Apple's own privacy terms. Apple Health data is managed by iOS and watchOS permissions and is not included in Hypertrove's TestFlight feedback unless you deliberately share it.

Retention and deletion

Hypertrove's app data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or remove the app. Removing the app may permanently remove local Hypertrove data. Because Hypertrove has no account or cloud backup, the developer cannot recover deleted data or remotely delete a copy stored on your device.

Children

Hypertrove is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Hypertrove changes. A future account, cloud-sync, analytics, or payment feature will require this policy and the app's privacy disclosures to be reviewed before release.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to hypertroveapp@gmail.com.
